Privacy policy - TV Dashboard

Last updated: 2026-11-20

What the app does

TV Dashboard is a personal app. A small server runs on the owner's own computer and feeds data to the owner's Samsung TV on the home network.

Google Calendar data

If the owner connects a Google account, the app requests read-only access to Google Calendar (scope calendar.readonly). It reads upcoming event titles, times and locations so they can be shown on the TV. The app does not create, change or delete calendar data.

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer, in a local file that is not shared. Calendar data is not sent to any other service, is not sold, and is not used for advertising or analytics.

The owner can revoke access at any time in their Google Account under Security > Third-party access, or by removing the app from the connected apps list.

Other data

To show stock quotes, weather, air quality and currency rates, the server asks public data services for those values. Weather requests include the coordinates of the chosen location. Portfolio holdings entered by the owner are stored locally on the owner's computer.

Data retention

Data stays on the owner's computer until deleted by the owner.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via GitHub Issues.
